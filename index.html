04 / The approach

From stuck to moving.

The work is tailored to the initiative, but the movement is consistent. Name the friction, align the people, and make the next step usable.

01 / Name the friction

See what is really in the way.

Surface the ownership gaps, conflicting assumptions, workflow problems, and adoption barriers hiding underneath the initiative.

02 / Align the people

Create shared language.

Bring leadership, technical teams, users, and stakeholders into a clearer conversation about what needs to happen next.

03 / Make it usable

Turn clarity into movement.

Translate decisions into roadmaps, requirements, designs, communication plans, and adoption practices teams can carry forward.

05 / About Upstart-Labs

The space between intent and impact.

Alexis Brochu is a UX product manager and AI enablement specialist with 20+ years of experience, PMP and Prosci CMP certified. She founded Upstart-Labs to help organizations navigate the human layer of complex product, technology, and organizational change.

She chairs Strategic AI Enablement for the New Hampshire Tech Alliance AI Task Force and co-hosts UXSG's weekly AIxUX meetup. Her work sits at the intersection of product strategy, UX research and design, change management, stakeholder alignment, and AI adoption. It is practical, human-centered, and built to help teams make better decisions together.
